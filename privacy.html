Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who we are. Nursery Rhymes World Z is a private tool owned by one individual. It is used only by its owner to upload the owner’s own educational videos for children to the owner’s own YouTube channel.

YouTube API Services. This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed. With the owner’s permission (OAuth), the app can upload videos and thumbnails and add videos to playlists on the owner’s channel. It also reads public video statistics to see which learning topics are popular.

Data stored. The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person, including viewers or children. Access tokens are kept only in the owner’s private, encrypted GitHub repository secrets. Only aggregated topic numbers are saved.

Revoking access. The owner can revoke this app’s access at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Data deletion. Because no personal data of other people is stored, there is nothing to delete. To request deletion of any information, contact us through the About page of our YouTube channel and it will be removed.

Feedback form. The feedback form on our website sends the message you type, and your name if you choose to add one, to the channel owner by email through the Web3Forms service. It is used only to read ideas and feedback. We do not ask for an email address and we do not reply.

Children. Videos are marked “Made for kids”. The app collects no data from children.

Contact. Use the feedback form on our website.
