Viewers

Short, cheerful songs that help toddlers and preschoolers learn English through singing.

  • Classic nursery rhymes, plus our own songs about brushing teeth, washing hands, colors and counting.
  • Words stay on screen so children can follow along and start to recognise them.
  • Every video is marked “Made for kids” on YouTube, so comments are off and ads are not personalised.
  • Happy endings only: when a rhyme has a fall or a fright, we add a kind verse so it ends well.
  • The songs, voices and 3D animation are made with AI tools by a small independent creator.
